## Hi, I'm Ho-jae.

I have spent my career standing between people who speak different languages,
whether those languages are Korean and English, military and civilian, or government and contractor.

I was:
1. **Interpreter (Republic of Korea Air Force, 2018 to 2021).** I interpreted for general officers in talks with foreign militaries.
2. **Translator (2021 to present).** Korean-to-English translation of scripts, proposals and fiction.
3. **Program officer, KOICA smart agriculture project in the Philippines (2025 to 2026).**
  I wrote a procurement manual from Philippine procurement law, caught an award-criteria error in a tender before it was published,
  and managed a baseline survey of 703 households.

That last job taught me something I did not expect. It's that good projects fail when the
infrastructure under them fails, and rules only protect people when someone checks that they are actually followed.

## Why cybersecurity

I want to work where policy meets infrastructure: writing security policies that work on real networks, and 
protecting the industrial systems (power, water, factories) that everyone depends on.
